browser_scene.xml adds a browser playground floor and simulation settings;
its STAND/SIT/FOLD keyframes are adapted from upstream scene_walk.xml.

The upstream model contains 14 articulated joints and does not model mouth
motor 34. The simulator's position actuators are an approximation; upstream
training uses BAM actuator models. The optional walking trial runs the
runtime's shipped ONNX policy in a separate native MuJoCo process on the UNO Q.

All runtime files are served locally. There are no CDN imports, analytics,
or external telemetry uploads. See vendor-lock.json for file checksums and
package provenance, and tools/prepare-browser-sim.py to reproduce the assets.
